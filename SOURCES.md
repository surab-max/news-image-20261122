## Lead — COP31 collapse (climate, Antalya)

- UN climate talks in Antalya, Türkiye end in collapse on Sunday
  with no overarching agreement, after running two days into
  overtime — the longest COP on record. The EU and others reject
  the final text put forward by COP31 presidents Türkiye and
  Australia because it makes no mention of fossil fuels.
  (Reuters via BusinessWorld, 22–23 Nov; edie, 22 Nov; BBC News,
  22 Nov 18:50 UTC: "COP31 fails to reach major climate deal";
  UN Secretary-General António Guterres: "deeply disappointed".)
- Australia's Chris Bowen calls the outcome "very disappointing";
  disagreements over fossil fuels and climate finance were the
  obstacles. Türkiye's Murat Kurum: "We did all that we could."

## Ukraine — Dymer, Kyiv region

- At least four people killed in Russian drone attacks on the
  Kyiv region on Sunday, President Zelenskiy and local officials
  say. A Russian drone partly collapses a five-storey apartment
  building in Dymer, north of Kyiv: three bodies found, about ten
  people may still be trapped under the rubble, nine evacuated,
  four injured including a teenager. Later Sunday, one person is
  killed when a drone strikes a grocery store in Vyshneve.
  (Reuters, Kyiv, 22 Nov 2026; BBC News, 22 Nov 16:37 UTC,
  Sarah Rainsford reporting from the collapsed building)

## Libya — Benghazi

## Indonesia — Banten factory fire

- A fire at the PT Fonusa Agung Mulia candy factory in Cikande,
  Serang, Banten (which makes Milkita sweets) kills at least
  three workers; rescuers search for 36 workers still missing.
  The blaze broke out Saturday evening; about 300 workers were
  inside, 15 survived (four injured). Police separately recovered
  seven body bags and put the unaccounted-for closer to 50, so
  the image uses the search agency's Sunday figures (3 dead,
  36 missing) given by Reuters / BBC that day.
  (Reuters, Jakarta, 22 Nov; BBC News, 22 Nov 14:05 UTC:
  "Three killed and dozens missing after fire at Indonesian
  factory"; AP, 22 Nov)

## Space — BepiColombo at Mercury (brief)

- The European–Japanese BepiColombo spacecraft begins orbiting
  Mercury after an eight-year journey — only the third spacecraft
  to visit the innermost planet. ESA confirms orbit on Saturday /
  reports it Sunday, and says a thruster glitch (a burn shorter
  than planned after a software issue) is being checked; the
  spacecraft is safe. Its two orbiters separate on 9–10 December.
  (Reuters, 22 Nov; AP via phys.org, 22 Nov; ESA Operations,
  22 Nov)
